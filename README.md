### T&O Bangalore · AI Innovation Lab Submission

> **Turning AI adoption into measurable business impact.**

---

## 1. Executive Summary

Organizations are rapidly adopting AI and automation to improve productivity, reduce manual effort, and accelerate business processes. However, measuring the actual business value generated after deployment remains a challenge.

The **AI Impact Tracker** is a lightweight solution designed to help teams measure and communicate the impact of AI and automation initiatives using simple, measurable before-and-after data.

Teams can record how long a task took before automation, how long it takes after automation, and how frequently the task is performed. The system then calculates the resulting **hours saved, projected annual impact, and estimated financial value**.

The goal is simple:

> **Move from “We implemented AI” to “Here is the measurable impact it created.”**

---

# 2. The Problem

Organizations are investing significantly in AI and automation, but deploying an AI solution is only the first step.

After implementation, teams may not have a simple and standardized way to answer questions such as:

* How much time did the AI solution actually save?
* How much manual effort was eliminated?
* Which automation initiatives are creating the greatest impact?
* What is the estimated business value generated?
* Which initiatives should be scaled or prioritized?
* Can the impact of different AI initiatives be compared consistently?

Without this visibility, the value of AI initiatives can be difficult to demonstrate to business leaders and decision-makers.

### Core Problem Statement

> **Organizations are adopting AI and automation faster than they can measure their impact, creating a gap between AI deployment and demonstrable business value.**

---

# 3. The Opportunity

Every AI or automation initiative has the potential to create measurable operational improvements.

For example:

### Before AI

An employee spends **12 minutes** processing one helpdesk ticket.

### After AI

AI-assisted automation reduces the effort to **2 minutes**.

### Weekly volume

The team processes **40 tickets per week**.

### Impact

**10 minutes saved per ticket**

× **40 tickets/week**

= **400 minutes/week**

= **6.67 hours saved per week**

Projected annually:

**6.67 × 52 = approximately 347 hours saved per year**

Instead of simply saying:

> “AI improved ticket processing.”

The team can now demonstrate:

> **“This automation saves approximately 347 working hours per year.”**

This is the type of measurable evidence the AI Impact Tracker is designed to provide.

---

# 4. Proposed Solution

## AI Impact Tracker

The AI Impact Tracker provides a simple mechanism for teams to record, measure, visualize, and compare the impact of their AI and automation initiatives.

### The basic concept:

**Before → After → Calculate → Visualize → Decide**

A team provides:

* Task name
* Business area
* Time required before automation
* Time required after automation
* Number of times the task is performed per week
* Optional hourly cost/value

The system automatically calculates:

* Hours saved per week
* Projected hours saved per year
* Estimated financial value
* Impact ranking
* Overall initiative impact

---

# 5. How It Works

## Step 1 — Identify an Automated Task

A team selects a task that has been improved using AI or automation.

Example:

**Task:** Invoice-PO Matching

---

## Step 2 — Enter Before & After Metrics

The user enters:

| Metric                 |    Example |
| ---------------------- | ---------: |
| Time Before Automation | 20 minutes |
| Time After Automation  |  5 minutes |
| Volume per Week        |   50 tasks |

---

## Step 3 — Calculate the Impact

The system calculates:

### Time saved per task

`20 − 5 = 15 minutes`

### Time saved per week

`15 × 50 = 750 minutes`

### Hours saved per week

`750 ÷ 60 = 12.5 hours`

### Projected annual hours saved

`12.5 × 52 = 650 hours`

The result is immediately displayed on the dashboard.

# 8. Measuring Business Value

The initial version focuses on measuring **time and productivity impact**.

The solution can be extended to estimate financial value.

For example:

**650 hours saved per year**

× **₹500 estimated value per hour**

= **₹3,25,000 estimated annual value**

This provides an additional business metric that can help teams communicate the potential value generated by an automation initiative.

> **Note:** Financial value is an estimate based on the assumptions entered by the user and should not automatically be treated as realized cost savings.
